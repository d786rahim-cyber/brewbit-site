Safety and smart-travel tips

Simple habits that save money and trouble.
  • Tip
    Enroll in STEP before any international trip

    The State Department's free Smart Traveler program emails embassy alerts for your destination and helps the embassy reach you in an emergency.

    US State Department →
  • Tip
    Buy entry permits only on government sites

    Look-alike sites charge several times the real fee for UK ETAs and EU permits. Skip sponsored search results and go to GOV.UK or the official EU site directly.

    GOV.UK →
  • Tip
    Get TSA PreCheck to keep shoes and laptop in place

    Enrolled travelers keep shoes, belts, laptops and liquids in their bags at screening. Enrollment costs $77.95 to $85 depending on the provider.

    TSA →
  • Tip
    Use Mobile Passport Control when flying home to DFW or Houston

    US citizens can submit passport details and customs answers in a free app, then show a QR receipt to the CBP officer. Both DFW and IAH take part.

    CBP →
