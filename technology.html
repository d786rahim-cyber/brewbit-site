Technology

Technology that keeps the plant running.

I work where business systems meet the factory floor. Good technology here is mostly invisible: production keeps moving, data is trusted, and security holds without slowing anyone down.

How I think about it

I start with the operation, not the tool. What does the plant need to run safely and on time, and where does technology get in the way? Then I pick the simplest design that is reliable, secure and supportable for years.

Most of the hard problems sit between systems: ERP to MES, IT to OT, cloud to plant. That is where I spend my time, connecting them cleanly and making sure someone can see when something breaks.

What I work with

Five areas, one plan

Manufacturing systems

MES, ERP and the shop floor

MES and OTOMES shop-floor systems connected to SAP, QAD, NetSuite and IQMS, so orders, production and traceability move as one flow.

Plant infrastructure

Networks that stay up

LAN, WAN, Wi-Fi and SD-WAN across plants and remote offices, with Fortinet firewalls, switching and segmented OT networks.

Cybersecurity

Protect the plant without stopping it

Zero Trust, privileged access, MFA and SSO, vulnerability management and IT/OT segmentation, guided by NIST and ISO 27001.

Cloud

Azure, AWS and Microsoft 365

Data center exits, migrations and hybrid environments, with backup, disaster recovery and cost control built in.

Data and AI

From the floor to the dashboard

Power BI manufacturing dashboards, real-time equipment data and AIOps monitoring that finds problems before users call.
