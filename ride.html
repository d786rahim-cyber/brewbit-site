Cycling

MS 150: Houston to Austin, 2018

The MS 150 is a two-day charity ride from Houston to Austin, held for the National MS Society. Riders raise money for research and for people living with multiple sclerosis, and the event is backed by sponsors from across the Texas energy industry.

I rode it in 2018. We started in Houston in the early morning, around 4:30 AM, and rode about 70 miles on the first day to the overnight stop in La Grange. The second day was about 80 miles into Austin. The event changed how I think about endurance and about riding for a cause.
